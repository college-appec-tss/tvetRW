ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About Rwanda TVET Student Hub

Rwanda TVET Student Hub is an independent educational website intended to make technical learning concepts easier to understand through clear explanations, examples and browser-based study tools.

Our approach

  • Explain concepts in beginner-friendly language.
  • Use original examples and practical exercises.
  • Review technical content for accuracy.
  • Make pages accessible and usable on mobile devices.

Independence and accuracy

This website is not an official government, examination-board or school website and does not claim affiliation with any institution. Course structures, assessment requirements and official results can change. Learners should confirm them with their school and relevant official sources.

Corrections

If you find an error or have a suggestion, use the contact page. Include the page title and a clear description of the issue.
