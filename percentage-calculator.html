Percentage calculator

Calculate what percentage one number is of another. Enter the part and the total to calculate the percentage.

Your result will appear here.

Formula

Percentage = (part ÷ total) × 100.

Example: 36 out of 50 is 72% because (36 ÷ 50) × 100 = 72.
