Weighted grade calculator

Use this tool to practise calculating a weighted score. Enter a mark and its weight for each assessment. The weights should add up to 100% for a complete result.

Your result will appear here.

Formula: (mark 1 × weight 1 + mark 2 × weight 2) ÷ 100. Confirm your institution's assessment rules before relying on any result.
