SUBJECT

Computer systems

A computer system combines hardware and software to receive, process, store and communicate information.

Topics to understand

  • CPU, RAM, motherboard and storage devices.
  • Input, output and peripheral devices.
  • Operating systems, filesystems and device drivers.
  • Boot process and basic troubleshooting.
  • Safe maintenance, backups and updates.

Practise by identifying the main components of a computer and explaining the role each one plays.
